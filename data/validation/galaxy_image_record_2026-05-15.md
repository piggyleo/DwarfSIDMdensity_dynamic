# Galaxy Image Record

Purpose: visual record of the 27 Hayashi et al. 2023 target galaxies. Coordinates are the current structural centers in `data/processed/galaxy_structural_centers.csv`.

Note: most panels are rendered/cropped from article PDFs or arXiv source figures. `Tucana 2` and `Triangulum II` are explicitly marked as project-generated fallback sky plots because no usable local article screenshot was available during this pass.
